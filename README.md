# Speed-reducer-spur-gear-for-conveyor-belt

# Design, Mathematical Computation, and SolidWorks Simulation of a Speed Reducer Spur Gearbox

## 1. Abstract
This project covers the end-to-end mechanical design, mathematical computation, and 3D CAD simulation of a high-reduction spur gearbox. The system was designed from scratch using analytical equations aligned with industrial standards and subsequently modeled and validated within **SolidWorks**. 

The final assembly delivers high torque transmission and extreme speed reduction tailored for continuous heavy-duty industrial conveyor systems.

---

## 2. Primary Goal & Target Specifications
The primary objective is to develop an optimized, high-efficiency spur gearbox engineered specifically for speed reduction on a food-processing conveyor belt.

### Operational Parameters
* **Application:** Food Industry Conveyor Belt System (Apple Transport Line)
* **Input Speed ($n_{in}$):** 5000 RPM
* **Target Output Speed ($n_{out}$):** 29 RPM *(Theoretical target: ~26 RPM)*
* **Conveyor Length:** 919 mm
* **Design Loading:** $2\text{ kg/m}^2$ standard compatibility up to $177\text{ kg/m}^2$ total localized loading capacity.

---

## 3. Engineering Research & Compliance Standards
To ensure structural integrity and compliance with manufacturing norms, material selection and gear geometry were evaluated using industrial datasets and standard design codes:

* **AGMA (American Gear Manufacturers Association):** Standardized stress analysis, bending strength, and surface durability (pitting resistance) calculations.
* **AISI / SAE Standards:** Material selection criteria focused on high-strength alloy steels to withstand continuous fatigue, surface wear, and high torque transmission.
* **Industrial Benchmarking:** Parametric values (module, pressure angle, face width, center distance) were cross-referenced against commercial industrial gearbox catalogs.

---

## 4. Key Design Features

* **Extreme Speed Reduction Ratio:** High step-down gear ratio designed to convert 5000 RPM motor input down to ~29 RPM output while maximizing continuous output torque.
* **Optimized Material Selection:** Designed with high-tensile alloy steels to maximize tooth fatigue strength and operational longevity under load.
* **Integrated Multi-Stage Architecture:** Multi-stage layout where intermediate gears function simultaneously as output and input stages within a compact footprint.
* **Full 3D CAD Parametric Assembly:** Complete modeling in SolidWorks including involute gear teeth profiles, keyed power transmission shafts, bearings, and protective casing.
* **FEA & Kinematic Validation:** Structural stress/strain distribution analysis, dynamic contact meshing evaluation, and motion simulations under realistic load scenarios.
